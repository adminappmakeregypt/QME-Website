How certification works

The same six steps apply to every standard. Your certificate is valid for three years and kept current through yearly audits.

  1. Application and quote

    Send us your activities, sites and number of employees. We reply with a proposal and audit duration.

  2. Stage 1 audit

    We review your documented system and confirm you are ready for the full audit.

  3. Stage 2 audit

    Our auditors check on site that the system is implemented and working.

  4. Certification decision

    The audit report is reviewed independently before the certificate is issued.

  5. Surveillance audits

    A yearly audit confirms the system is maintained and improving.

  6. Recertification

    A full audit before the three-year cycle ends renews your certificate.

Common questions

What is the difference between certification and accreditation?

Certification is what your organization receives after a successful audit. Accreditation is what a certification body like QME holds, granted by a national authority such as EGAC, to show it audits competently and impartially.

How long is an ISO certificate valid?

Three years, as long as the yearly surveillance audits are passed. A recertification audit before expiry starts the next cycle.

Can QME help us build the system and then certify it?

No. To stay impartial, a certification body does not consult for the organizations it certifies. We can train your team, and we audit the system you build.
